# Judge panel on the five concepts

Four judge lenses scored five independent concepts (23 October 2026). Aggregate: innovator 145, storyteller 145, ship-it-realist 143, judge-maximizer 140, business-builder 140. The final spec is the innovator spine with the storyteller's video moments grafted on and the realist's kinematic fallback kept.
